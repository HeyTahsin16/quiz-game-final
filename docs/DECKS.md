# Quiz Duel — Character Deck Recipes & Balance Summary

Each of the 4 playable characters uses a fixed 24-card deck (all cards start un-upgraded in Frame A) satisfying:
- **Rarity split**: 10 Normal, 8 Uncommon, 4 Epic, 2 Legendary
- **Copy limits**: Normal <= 3, Uncommon <= 2, Epic <= 1, Legendary <= 1
- **Subject split**: 10 in character's affinity (+25% outgoing, -25% incoming), 2 in character's weakness (-25% outgoing, +25% incoming), and 6 + 6 in the two neutral subjects.

## 1. Dark Ninja (`dark-ninja`)

- **Normal (10)**: `C1` Deadly Poison ×2, `C2` Poisoned Stab ×2, `C6` Bane ×2, `M1` Strike ×2, `B1` Bandage Up, `B5` Antidote
