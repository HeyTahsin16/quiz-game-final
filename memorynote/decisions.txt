# Quiz Duel — Architecture & Design Decisions Log (Append-Only)
2026-10-24: Initialized decisions.txt for Phase 0 pre-flight.
2026-10-24 [Phase 0 Decisions & Defaults]:
1. Reference code location: The workspace root already contains `oskarrough/slaytheweb` (`src/game`, `src/content`, `tests`), so no zip extraction is needed; engine files will be ported to `src/engine/` with AGPL-3.0 headers and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
2. Accessibility vs Quiz Text Protection (Section 7): Question and answer text will be rendered strictly to `<canvas>` with jitter/scanline/watermark and no DOM/ARIA fallback, trading screen-reader accessibility for anti-copy friction as required by Section 7.
3. Affinity multipliers on multi-subject interactions (Section 4 & 5): Outgoing affinity (`x1.25` if card.subject === attacker.affinity, `x0.75` if card.subject === attacker.weakness) and incoming affinity (`x0.75` if card.subject === defender.affinity, `x1.25` if card.subject === defender.weakness) both multiply in the single damage pipeline before Block.
4. Damage rounding (Section 5): Round at the end of the damage multiplier chain (`Math.round(Math.max(0, (base + strength * mult) * outAffinity * statusMod * inAffinity * quizScale))`) before subtracting Block; if epic/legendary reduced form (`quizScale = 0.35`) is active on a card with positive base damage, minimum pre-block damage is `1`.
5. Poison vs Block & Ward (Section 5 & Keywords): Poison and Parasite cause direct HP loss (`loses N HP`) at turn start, bypassing Block. Ward negates incoming debuff applications (1 Ward consumed per debuff type applied on a card).
6. Round vs Turn durations (Section 5 & Keywords): 1 round = 1 turn per player. Duration-based statuses (`weak`, `vulnerable`, `corrode`, `antiHeal`, `hemostasis`, `regen`) decrement at the end of the affected player's turn (or start of turn for `regen`/`poison`/`parasite` after ticking).
7. Max HP increases (`M20 Feed`, `B14 Growth Spurt`): `M20 Feed` raises `maxHealth` by 4 (and heals via its 50% life steal); `B14 Growth Spurt` raises `maxHealth` by 4 and heals 6.
8. Upgrade rule for all 80 cards (Section 6): Cards with cost >= 2 that have no numeric magnitude (e.g. `P16 Barricade`, `P19 Apotheosis`, `C18 Paralytic Gas`, `B19 Phoenix`) upgrade by `-1 cost`; numeric cards upgrade their primary numbers by `+40%` (rounded, at least +1).
9. Deck construction (Section 4): Each character's 24-card deck strictly follows 10 affinity / 2 weakness / 6 other / 6 other subjects and 10 normal (<=3 copies each) / 8 uncommon (<=2 copies each) / 4 epic (unique) / 2 legendary (unique).
10. Role-lint boundary (Section 6): Role lint enforces the "Cannot" column strictly (Maths: no block, no non-lifesteal heal; Physics: no life steal, no reflect; Chemistry: no direct damage, no heal, no block; Biology: no damage) so `M20` (+max HP), `P18` (HP cost), and `B12` (draw) pass as valid catalog cards.
2026-10-24 [Phase 1 Asset Provisioning]: Because the external Google Drive link requires Google authentication from inside the container, generated deterministic pixel-art PNG assets (`20240707dragon9SlicesA.png`, `20240707dragon9SlicesB.png`, 4 character 4-frame sprite sheets) and 3 formant-synthesized male WAV voice packs (`voice-dark`, `voice-heroic`, `voice-stoic` x 5 events) at `/public/assets/`, with one-line config paths in `src/engine/config.ts` so user files dropped into `/public/assets/` work without code changes.
- Phase 2: Hand persistence and turn-start draw: Players start with 5 cards and 5 mana. Because mana and hands persist across turns (unlike standard Slay the Spire where hands are discarded each turn), at the start of each subsequent turn a player gains +1 mana (clamped to 5) and draws 1 card if their hand has fewer than 5 cards (`handSize = 5`), while card-draw effects (`Pommel Strike`, `Shrug It Off`, `Offering`, `Metabolism`) draw up to `maxHandSize = 7`.
- Phase 2: Round-decaying status durations (`weak`, `vulnerable`, `corrode`, `antiHeal`, `noLifeSteal`, `stunImmune`) decrement by 1 at the end of the affected player's turn so a 1-round debuff applied by the opponent always lasts through the target's upcoming turn.
